# Frameworks

One card per engine this product actually runs: what it is, why it was chosen, the exact pin, how it is used
here, and the license of its code and of its weights. A card appears when its engine lands.

- [01, flyvis](frameworks/01_flyvis.md): connectome-constrained networks, the engine this product builds its
  connectome for
- [02, OpenCV](frameworks/02_opencv.md): decoding TartanAir as its reference reader does, resampling every
  frame to the lattice's geometry, warping panoramas and exposures
- [03, FlyGym and MuJoCo](frameworks/03_flygym.md): the fly's own compound eye, and the scenes of the
  ethological cases rendered through it

Planned cards, in the order their units arrive: PyTorch, scikit-image, the depth and segmentation engines of
the method ladder, and the browser runtime.
