# M04, the published network with its own flow decoder

**Consumes** the 721 luminances and the camera motion, exactly as M01 and M03 do. It is the method this
product generalises, used as published and frozen, so the difference between this row and M01 is the
source of the flow and nothing else. That is the comparison this row exists to make.

## What it is

The fifty-model ensemble of Lappalainen et al. (2024), every parameter frozen, with the `DecoderGAVP` head
it was trained with (`flyvis/config/task/task.yaml`: shape [8, 2], kernel 5, constant weight 0.001, a
hex-space convolution stack ending in two output channels). The decoder emits a flow vector per column, in
the engine's own units, which is the unit the corpus already stores its flow in: pixels per image height,
y up, summed over the 13 x 13 box. That flow goes straight into the shared parallax inversion.

## Two things stated rather than smoothed over

**It was trained at Sintel's frame rate.** The network is simulated at the task's own step (`dt` 0.02 s),
and a clip of another source is resampled to that step by holding each frame for its own interval. A
TartanAir clip at 0.1 s per frame is therefore five simulated steps per frame, against roughly two for the
Sintel frames the model was trained on. The report records the interval each clip was run at.

**The ensemble is fifty models.** M04 reports the median across the models it was run with and their
spread; a single model is never the headline. Running all fifty on every clip is expensive, so the number
of models is a parameter, and the report records how many were used.

## What it measured

On a synthetic clip of planes at a known depth, its decoded flow is a third of the true displacement:
0.87 pixels against 2.73. Over the whole matrix, the median over each case's six levels:

| Case | Coverage | AbsRel |
|---|---|---|
| C01 forest flight | 0.49 | 1.124 |
| C02 urban street | 0.51 | 0.840 |
| C03 hospital corridor | 0.53 | 1.790 |
| C05 city at night | 0.49 | 1.462 |
| C06 motion blur | 0.55 | 1.071 |
| C07 marsh in fog | 0.47 | 0.960 |
| C15 textured planes | 0.41 | 6.336 |
| C16 textureless surfaces | 0.27 | 1.926 |
| C13 pure rotation | refused 1.00 | not applicable |
| C14 static camera | refused 1.00 | not applicable |

Paired against the floor over the 376 clips both rows cover, the difference in AbsRel is **0.894, with a
95 percent interval of 0.865 to 0.957**, which is within the interval of M03, an untrained correlator with
three rings of pooling and one fitted gain. On this corpus, used as a metric flow source outside the frame
rate and scene statistics it was trained on, the published model is no better than that.

The reading is not that the published model is poor. It is that a model trained on Sintel at 24 frames per
second, decoding flow for a scene statistic and a frame rate it never saw, does not transfer as a metric
flow source to this corpus. Whether a network built from the MaleCNS connectome and trained on THIS corpus
does better is the question the units above this one exist to answer, and this row is the reference they
are read against.
