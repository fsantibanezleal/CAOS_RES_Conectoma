# M02, stereo by semi-global matching. An upper bound

**Consumes** the full 640 x 640 pixel grid of two cameras 0.25 metres apart. That is a camera rig, not a
fly, and it is in the ladder for exactly that reason: it bounds what a classical geometric method gets
from these scenes. Everywhere M02 appears it is labelled an upper bound, and the gap between it and the
native rows is a result rather than something to apologise for.

**Computes** a planar depth per column, from disparity, with no motion involved at all. It says nothing
about moving objects: stereo cannot, from one instant.

## How

The matcher is Hirschmueller's semi-global matching as OpenCV implements it
(`cv2.StereoSGBM_create`, 160 disparities, block 5, the usual smoothness penalties). The geometry is the
release's own, read from tartanair.org/modalities.html on 2026-09-22: two stereo sets of six cameras,
baseline 0.25 m, pinhole, 640 x 640, 90 degree field of view, focal length 320 pixels, principal point
(320, 320). The pair is rectified by construction, being a synthetic rig, so nothing here rectifies
anything and no rectification is invented.

```
depth = focal * baseline / disparity
```

The depth map is then sampled onto the lattice by the same box rule the ground truth uses, the median over
each column's 13 x 13 box, so M02's numbers live in the same space as every other row's. A column whose
box holds no valid disparity is refused, not filled.

## What it can and cannot be run on

The corpus fetched the left front camera only, because that is the camera the release distributes optical
flow for. The right camera of the 48 TartanAir clips the cases draw was fetched for this method (0.87 GB,
nothing committed).

A case level that changes the left image has to change the right image the same way. A gain and photon
noise can be reproduced from the image alone; fog needs the right camera's depth and exposure blur needs
its flow, and neither was fetched. Those levels are reported as `skipped` with the reason rather than run
on a mismatched pair. Cases from any other source have no second camera at all.

## What it measured

Over every level, eight clips each, against the depth the corpus committed:

| Case | Coverage | AbsRel | delta below 1.25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 forest flight | 0.42 | 0.054 | 0.946 |
| C02 urban street | 0.57 | 0.054 | 0.969 |
| C03 hospital corridor | 0.54 | 0.013 | 0.996 |
| C05 city at night | 0.30 | 0.017 | 0.992 |

Paired against the floor over the 188 clips both rows cover, the median difference in AbsRel is 0.004,
with a 95 percent interval of 0.000 to 0.008: a stereo rig with a quarter-metre baseline and a full pixel
grid is, on these scenes, as good as inverting the flow the dataset itself provides. That is the bound the
native rows are read against.
